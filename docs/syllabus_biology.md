# Biology Syllabus — Class 12 NCERT (13 Chapters)

## B1 — Reproduction in Organisms
- Reproduction: definition; types (asexual and sexual)
- Asexual reproduction: binary fission, budding, fragmentation, spore formation, vegetative propagation
- Sexual reproduction: events (pre-fertilization, fertilization, post-fertilization); gametogenesis; gamete transfer
- Lifecycle and alternation of generations

## B2 — Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
- Flower structure: parts and functions; stamen and pistil
- Microsporogenesis: anther structure; microsporangium; pollen grain development (2-celled and 3-celled)
- Megasporogenesis: ovule structure; embryo sac development (Polygonum type); types of ovules
- Pollination: types (self and cross); agents; adaptations; outbreeding devices
- Pollen-pistil interaction; fertilization: double fertilization; syngamy and triple fusion
- Post-fertilization: endosperm development (nuclear, cellular, helobial); embryo development (dicot and monocot); seed and fruit development; apomixis and polyembryony

## B3 — Human Reproduction
- Male reproductive system: structure and function; spermatogenesis (spermatogonia → primary spermatocyte → secondary spermatocyte → spermatid → sperm); sperm structure
- Female reproductive system: structure and function; oogenesis (oogonia → primary oocyte → secondary oocyte → ovum); follicular development; ovulation
- Menstrual cycle: phases (menstrual, follicular, ovulatory, luteal); hormonal regulation (FSH, LH, estrogen, progesterone)
- Fertilization: events; formation of zygote; cleavage; morula; blastocyst; implantation
- Pregnancy and embryonic development: placenta; gastrulation; organogenesis; parturition

## B4 — Reproductive Health
- Reproductive health: definition; problems and strategies
- Population explosion: causes; measures
- Contraception: natural methods; barrier methods; IUDs; hormonal contraceptives (oral pills, injections); surgical methods (vasectomy, tubectomy)
- Medical termination of pregnancy (MTP)
- Sexually transmitted infections (STIs/STDs): types, symptoms, prevention (HIV/AIDS, gonorrhoea, syphilis, warts, hepatitis B)
- Infertility: causes; assisted reproductive technologies (ART): IVF, ZIFT, GIFT, ICSI, surrogacy

## B5 — Principles of Inheritance and Variation
- Mendel's laws: law of dominance; law of segregation; law of independent assortment; experimental evidence
- Inheritance of one gene: monohybrid cross; test cross; back cross; chi-square test
- Inheritance of two genes: dihybrid cross; independent assortment
- Deviations from Mendelism: incomplete dominance; co-dominance; multiple alleles (ABO blood groups); pleiotropy; polygenic inheritance
- Chromosomal theory of inheritance; linkage and crossing over
- Sex determination: sex chromosomes in humans (XX/XY); sex-linked inheritance (haemophilia, colour blindness; fruit fly examples)
- Pedigree analysis; Mendelian disorders (thalassaemia, sickle cell anaemia, phenylketonuria, haemophilia, colour blindness)
- Chromosomal disorders: Down syndrome (trisomy 21); Turner's syndrome (45, X); Klinefelter's syndrome (47, XXY)

## B6 — Molecular Basis of Inheritance
- DNA: structure (Griffith, Avery, MacLeod, McCarty experiments; Hershey-Chase experiment; Watson-Crick double helix); Chargaff's rules; packaging of DNA (nucleosome, chromatin, chromosome)
- Replication: semi-conservative replication (Meselson-Stahl experiment); enzymes (helicase, SSB, primase, DNA pol I, III, ligase); leading and lagging strand; Okazaki fragments
- Transcription: RNA polymerase; promoter; template strand; transcription unit; hnRNA; processing (5' cap, poly-A tail, splicing)
- Genetic code: codons; codon table; wobble hypothesis; properties of genetic code; mutation
- Translation: ribosomes (structure: 70S, 80S); tRNA (anticodon, amino acid attachment); steps (initiation, elongation, termination); post-translational modifications
- Gene regulation: lac operon (negative control); gene regulation in eukaryotes (overview)
- Human Genome Project: salient features; applications; implications
- DNA fingerprinting: VNTR; technique; applications (forensics, paternity testing)

## B7 — Evolution
- Origin of life: Oparin-Haldane hypothesis; Miller-Urey experiment; protocells
- Evidence for evolution: morphological (homologous and analogous organs); palaeontological; embryological; molecular (DNA, protein)
- Theories of evolution: Lamarckism; Darwinism (natural selection; fitness; adaptation); Neo-Darwinism (modern synthesis)
- Mechanism of evolution: mutation; gene flow; genetic drift (bottleneck effect; founder effect); natural selection (types: directional, stabilizing, disruptive)
- Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium: principle; conditions; violation factors; allele frequency calculation
- Speciation: types (allopatric, sympatric); reproductive isolation (pre-zygotic, post-zygotic)
- Human evolution: Dryopithecus; Ramapithecus; Australopithecus; Homo habilis; Homo erectus; Homo sapiens; Neanderthals; Cro-Magnon

## B8 — Human Health and Disease
- Health: WHO definition; factors affecting health; concept of immunity
- Innate immunity: barriers (physical, physiological, cellular, cytokine); types of immune cells
- Acquired immunity: humoral (antibodies: structure, types) and cell-mediated immunity; active and passive immunity; memory cells
- Vaccination: principle; types; common vaccines
- Common diseases: typhoid (Salmonella typhi); pneumonia (Streptococcus pneumoniae); common cold (rhino virus); amoebiasis; malaria (life cycle of Plasmodium; Anopheles mosquito); ascariasis; ringworm
- AIDS: HIV structure; HIV replication; transmission; prevention; ELISA diagnosis; HAART treatment
- Cancer: types (benign, malignant); causes; metastasis; treatment (surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, immunotherapy)
- Drugs and alcohol abuse: effects; addiction; withdrawal; prevention; treatment

## B9 — Microbes in Human Welfare
- Beneficial microbes in food production: Lactobacillus (curd); yeast (bread, ethanol); cheese production; single cell protein (SCP)
- Industrial applications: biogas production; BOD and sewage treatment (primary and secondary treatment); biocontrol agents
- Antibiotics: penicillin discovery; action of antibiotics
- Bioactive molecules: cyclosporin A (Trichoderma polysporum); statins (Monascus purpureus)
- Biofertilisers: Rhizobium; blue-green algae (Anabaena, Nostoc); Azolla; mycorrhizae

## B10 — Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
- Principles of biotechnology: genetic engineering; bioprocess engineering
- Tools of recombinant DNA technology: restriction endonucleases (RE: types I, II, III); gel electrophoresis; cloning vectors (pBR322; BACs; YACs; Ti plasmid; retroviruses); polymerase chain reaction (PCR: denaturation, annealing, extension; primers; Taq polymerase)
- Process of recombinant DNA technology: isolation of DNA; restriction digestion; gel electrophoresis; elution; ligation; transformation (CaCl₂, electroporation, biolistics, microinjection); selection and screening (antibiotic resistance, blue-white selection)
- Bioreactors: types; components; stirred tank; aeration and agitation; scale-up

## B11 — Biotechnology and Its Applications
- Applications in agriculture: Bt cotton (Cry proteins; Agrobacterium-mediated transformation); RNA interference (RNAi); transgenic plants
- Applications in medicine: genetically engineered insulin (Eli Lilly); gene therapy (ADA deficiency); molecular diagnosis (PCR, ELISA, DNA probes)
- Transgenic animals: Rosie (human protein in milk); knock-out mice; safety evaluation of vaccines; chemical sensitivity; disease models
- Ethical issues: biopiracy; patents; biodiversity conservation; biosafety regulations (Cartagena Protocol)

## B12 — Organisms and Populations
- Organism and its environment: major abiotic factors; habitat and niche; adaptation
- Abiotic factors: temperature; water; light; soil; responses to abiotic factors (regulators, conformers, migrators, suspenders)
- Population: attributes (birth rate, death rate, age ratio, sex ratio); growth models (exponential and logistic); K and r strategies
- Population interactions: mutualism; commensalism; parasitism; competition; predation; amensalism; neutralism; examples of each

## B13 — Ecosystem
- Ecosystem: structure; function; energy flow (food chains, food webs, ecological pyramids: number, biomass, energy)
- Productivity: GPP; NPP; secondary productivity; standing crop; biomass
- Decomposition: process; factors; humus; mineralisation
- Nutrient cycling: carbon cycle; phosphorus cycle
- Ecological succession: primary and secondary; pioneer species; climax community; hydrosere; xerosere
- Ecosystem services: definition; provisioning; regulating; cultural; supporting services; value of biodiversity
- Biodiversity and Conservation (B13 extension):
  - Biodiversity: levels (genetic, species, ecosystem); patterns (latitudinal gradients; species-area relationship)
  - Biodiversity loss: causes (habitat destruction, invasive species, over-exploitation, co-extinctions — HIPPO)
  - Conservation: in-situ (national parks, biosphere reserves, wildlife sanctuaries, hotspots) and ex-situ (zoos, seed banks, tissue culture, cryopreservation)
  - International agreements: Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD); CITES
