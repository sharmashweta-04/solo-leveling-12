# Chemistry Syllabus — Class 12 NCERT (10 Chapters)

## C1 — Solutions
- Types of solutions; concentration terms: molarity, molality, mole fraction, ppm
- Solubility: gases in liquids (Henry's law); solids in liquids
- Vapour pressure of liquid solutions; Raoult's law; ideal and non-ideal solutions
- Colligative properties: relative lowering of vapour pressure, elevation of boiling point, depression of freezing point, osmotic pressure
- Determination of molecular mass using colligative properties; abnormal molecular masses; van't Hoff factor

## C2 — Electrochemistry
- Electrochemical cells: Galvanic cell; Daniell cell; electrode potential; standard electrode potential
- Nernst equation; cell EMF; Gibbs energy and EMF
- Conductance in electrolytic solutions: specific conductance, molar conductance; Kohlrausch's law
- Electrolysis; Faraday's laws of electrolysis; lead storage battery; fuel cells
- Corrosion: mechanism; prevention

## C3 — Chemical Kinetics
- Rate of a reaction; rate law; order and molecularity of reactions
- Integrated rate equations (zero order, first order); half-life
- Pseudo first order reactions; temperature dependence of rate constant
- Arrhenius equation: activation energy, frequency factor; effect of catalyst

## C4 — d- and f-Block Elements
- General introduction; electronic configuration; occurrence; oxidation states
- Properties of transition metals: variation in atomic and ionic radii, ionisation energy, oxidation states, colour, magnetic properties, catalytic behaviour, alloy formation, interstitial compounds
- Important compounds of transition metals: KMnO₄, K₂Cr₂O₇
- Lanthanoids: electronic configuration; oxidation states; lanthanoid contraction
- Actinoids: electronic configuration; oxidation states; comparison with lanthanoids

## C5 — Coordination Compounds
- Ligands; coordination number; coordination sphere; central atom; oxidation number
- Nomenclature of coordination compounds
- Isomerism: structural (ionisation, linkage, coordination, solvate); stereo (geometrical, optical)
- Bonding: Werner's theory; valence bond theory; crystal field theory; colour in coordination compounds; magnetic properties
- Importance and applications of coordination compounds (biological systems, analytical chemistry, metallurgy, catalysis)

## C6 — Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
- Classification and nomenclature
- Nature of C–X bond; properties; mechanism of substitution (SN1 and SN2)
- Physical properties of haloalkanes and haloarenes
- Chemical reactions: substitution, elimination; reaction with metals
- Polyhalogen compounds: dichloromethane, chloroform, iodoform, carbon tetrachloride, DDT and its consequences

## C7 — Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers
- Classification and nomenclature
- Methods of preparation of alcohols (from alkenes, carbonyl compounds, Grignard reagent), phenols (from benzene sulphonic acid, diazonium salts, cumene process), ethers (dehydration of alcohols, Williamson's synthesis)
- Physical and chemical properties of alcohols, phenols and ethers
- Reactions: esterification, oxidation; Lucas test; iodoform test; Williamson synthesis

## C8 — Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids
- Nomenclature and nature of carbonyl group
- Preparation of aldehydes and ketones (from alcohols, alkenes, Grignard reagent, Friedel-Crafts acylation)
- Physical and chemical properties: nucleophilic addition, aldol condensation, Cannizzaro reaction, oxidation, reduction
- Carboxylic acids: nomenclature, preparation, physical and chemical properties
- Acidity; reactions: RCOOR' esterification, Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky reaction, Rosenmund reduction, Gattermann Koch reaction

## C9 — Amines
- Classification and nomenclature
- Structure of amines; preparation: reduction of nitro compounds, Gabriel phthalimide synthesis, Hofmann bromamide reaction
- Physical properties; chemical properties: basic character; alkylation; acylation; carbylamine reaction; coupling reaction
- Diazonium salts: preparation, properties and importance in synthetic organic chemistry

## C10 — Biomolecules
- Carbohydrates: classification (monosaccharides, disaccharides, polysaccharides); open chain and cyclic structure of glucose; D-L configuration; anomers; epimers; Haworth projections; fructose; sucrose; starch; cellulose; glycogen; functions
- Proteins: amino acids (essential and non-essential); peptide bond; classification of proteins (primary, secondary, tertiary, quaternary structure); denaturation; enzymes; mechanism of enzyme action; cofactors, coenzymes
- Lipids: fatty acids; triglycerides; phospholipids; waxes; steroids
- Nucleic acids: DNA double helix (Watson-Crick model); RNA types; genetic code; transcription and translation overview
- Vitamins: fat-soluble (A, D, E, K) and water-soluble (B group, C); deficiency diseases
- Hormones: classification and overview (steroid, amino acid derived, peptide hormones)
