# Physics Syllabus — Class 12 NCERT (14 Chapters)

## P1 — Electric Charges and Fields
- Electric charge, properties of charge (conservation, quantization, additivity)
- Coulomb's law; force between multiple charges; superposition principle
- Electric field: concept, field lines, uniform and non-uniform fields
- Electric dipole: field on axial and equatorial points; torque on dipole in uniform field
- Gauss's law: statement, proof, applications (infinite plane sheet, spherical shell, line charge)
- Continuous charge distributions: linear, surface, volume

## P2 — Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance
- Electric potential: definition, relationship with electric field, potential due to point charge, dipole, system of charges
- Equipotential surfaces; potential energy of system of charges; potential energy in external field
- Conductors: electrostatics of conductors; electrostatic shielding
- Dielectrics and polarization; capacitors and capacitance; parallel plate capacitor
- Effect of dielectric on capacitance; combination of capacitors (series, parallel)
- Energy stored in capacitor; van de Graaff generator

## P3 — Current Electricity
- Electric current; drift velocity; mobility; Ohm's law; electrical resistance; V-I characteristics
- Temperature dependence of resistance; electrical energy and power
- Carbon resistors; colour coding; series and parallel combinations
- Internal resistance; EMF; terminal potential difference; Kirchhoff's laws
- Wheatstone bridge; metre bridge; potentiometer (EMF comparison; internal resistance)

## P4 — Moving Charges and Magnetism
- Concept of magnetic field; Oersted's experiment
- Biot-Savart law: magnetic field due to current element, circular coil (axial), long straight wire
- Ampere's circuital law; solenoid; toroid
- Force on moving charge in magnetic field; cyclotron
- Force on current-carrying conductor; force between two parallel conductors; definition of ampere
- Torque on current loop; magnetic dipole; moving coil galvanometer (conversion to ammeter/voltmeter)

## P5 — Magnetism and Matter
- Bar magnet as equivalent solenoid; magnetic field lines; magnetic moment
- Torque and potential energy of magnetic dipole in uniform field
- Gauss's law in magnetism
- Magnetization and magnetic intensity; magnetic susceptibility and permeability
- Diamagnetism, paramagnetism, ferromagnetism; hysteresis; permanent magnets and electromagnets

## P6 — Electromagnetic Induction
- Faraday's laws of electromagnetic induction; Lenz's law
- Motional EMF; magnetic flux; eddy currents; inductance (mutual and self)
- AC generator

## P7 — Alternating Current
- AC voltage, current, peak and RMS values
- AC through resistor, inductor and capacitor (phasor diagrams)
- Series LCR circuit; resonance; quality factor; power factor; power in AC circuits
- LC oscillations; transformers

## P8 — Electromagnetic Waves
- Displacement current; Ampere-Maxwell law
- EM waves: sources, nature, transverse character, speed in vacuum
- EM spectrum: radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays; uses of each

## P9 — Ray Optics and Optical Instruments
- Reflection: laws, spherical mirrors, mirror formula, magnification
- Refraction: Snell's law, critical angle, TIR; apparent depth
- Refraction through prism; dispersion; rainbow; scattering
- Lenses: thin lens formula, magnification, lens maker's equation; power of lens; combination of thin lenses
- Eye and defects of vision; simple microscope; compound microscope; astronomical telescope (reflecting and refracting)

## P10 — Wave Optics
- Wave front; Huygens' principle; laws of reflection and refraction from Huygens' principle
- Interference: Young's double slit experiment; coherent sources; fringe width; sustained interference
- Diffraction: single slit; Rayleigh criterion; resolving power of microscope and telescope
- Polarization: Brewster's law; polaroids; use of polaroids

## P11 — Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter
- Photoelectric effect; Einstein's photoelectric equation; work function; threshold frequency
- de Broglie hypothesis; wavelength of matter waves; Davisson-Germer experiment
- Particle nature of light; wave-particle duality; Heisenberg uncertainty principle

## P12 — Atoms
- Alpha particle scattering experiment; Rutherford's nuclear model
- Atomic spectra; Bohr model of hydrogen atom: postulates, energy levels, spectral series
- Line spectra of hydrogen; spectral series formula
- Limitations of Bohr model

## P13 — Nuclei
- Composition of nucleus; nuclear size; nuclear density; nuclear force
- Mass defect; binding energy per nucleon; nuclear stability
- Radioactivity: alpha, beta, gamma decay; law of radioactive decay; half-life; mean life
- Nuclear reactions; nuclear fission; chain reaction; nuclear reactor; nuclear fusion

## P14 — Semiconductor Electronics: Materials, Devices and Simple Circuits
- Energy bands in solids; intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors; p-type and n-type
- p-n junction; depletion layer; barrier potential; p-n junction diode (forward and reverse bias)
- Rectifiers: half-wave and full-wave; diode as rectifier
- Special purpose diodes: LED, photodiode, solar cell, Zener diode (as voltage regulator)
- Transistor: structure (NPN/PNP); working in common emitter configuration; characteristics
- Transistor as switch; transistor as amplifier (CE mode); transistor as oscillator
- Logic gates: OR, AND, NOT, NAND, NOR; boolean expressions; De Morgan's theorems
